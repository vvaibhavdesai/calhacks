## Design, in brief

- **Cumulative, idempotent posting.** Each business fact has a
  deterministic base key, e.g. `SALES:<vendor>:<date>:1100`,
  `COMMISSION:<vendor>:<date>:<root settlement>`,
  `SUPPLIER_INVOICE:<id>`. Re-posting computes the delta against every
  entry already posted under that key. No delta means a no-op. A non-zero
  delta appends `<key>#adjN` with `correction_of` set to the original entry,
  which is never rewritten. A per-key `pg_advisory_xact_lock` serializes
  concurrent posts of the same fact.
- **Organizer revenue is not vendor gross sales.** Sales capture credits
  vendor funds payable, tax and tips. Commission is recognized only when the
  settlement posts. Vendor-retained sales post nothing (C02 fixture 4).
- **Closed days.** After `POST /close`, a *new* fact dated on that day is
  refused with 409 `business_date_closed`. A late change to an
  already-posted fact (a late refund, a settlement correction) still posts
  as a linked `#adj` entry noted `posted_after_close`, because refusing it
  would hide a real fact. The close snapshot itself stays as it was.
- **Payouts post on confirmation**, using the festival-local date of the
  confirmation. The 10:00 payout for night N lands on N+1, after N may
  already be closed.
- **Cash.** The journal is the source of truth. `freely_available = bank −
  protected (tax + tips + customer refunds) − vendor funds payable −
  supplier AP due within the horizon`. A negative value is shown as a
  shortfall, not hidden. A07's `cash_pools` stays the atomic spend guard:
  vendor releases reserve from it, and supplier payments now draw from the
  same locked row. The cash report shows the pool beside the books and sets
  `exceeds_book_spendable` when a finance lead has made more available than
  the books support.

## Implemented vs fixture-only vs incomplete

- **Implemented:** everything in the table above, over HTTP.
- **Fixture-only:** orders, payments, the processor deposit and settlements
  are inserted directly by `dev_fixtures_a08.py`, with settlement numbers
  from A04's real engine. Ingesting the generator's suppliers/POs/invoices/
  tickets/sponsors/expenses JSON through these endpoints is not built.
- **Conservative defaults (not approved business facts):**
  - The DEMO tax config (9.25% prepared food and merch, packaged food
    exempt, central tax owned by the organizer as agent, vendor-retained tax
    owned by the vendor) is versioned and replaceable via
    `PUT /finance/config`.
  - The forecast horizon defaults to 14 days.
  - Refund tax/tips are allocated proportionally from the order
    (ROUND_HALF_UP) and split across lines by largest remainder, ties broken
    by line ID (C01).
  - Sponsorship is recognized on receipt, and unpaid sponsor invoices are a
    memo receivable.
  - Ticket revenue is deferred until an explicit `tickets/recognize`.
  - Daily-close hard checks: trial balance, every entry balanced, AP ties,
    tax ties, all settlements posted. Warnings: suspense non-zero, missing
    statements, processor clearing non-zero.
- **Incomplete / known gaps:**
  - Tips are not passed through to vendors. A04's `payout_cents` excludes
    tips, so tips stay in TIPS_LIABILITY as protected cash. The oracle pays
    tips with the payout. **This needs a decision** before scoring.
  - Cash-office deposits (cash office → bank) and cash over/short postings
    aren't built, because there is no deposit-slip model on `lane`.
  - Unmatched bank entries are not posted to suspense.
  - There is no recovery/offset workflow for a vendor who owes money back
    after a late refund. The debit balance is visible in vendor payable and
    the cash report, but the collection itself is A09.
  - Money on the wire is integer cents, consistent with existing A-track
    endpoints, not C01's base-10 string. That should be a deliberate
    cross-cutting change, not one router's.
  - Bank-date vs business-date: postings use the bank entry's
    `business_date` as stored by A03.
