# Vendor Participation Agreement: Taco Libre

FICTIONAL DOCUMENT - synthetic test data for the Encore demo. Not a real agreement.

- Agreement version: 1
- Festival: Golden Coast Music Festival (fictional), 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-18, America/Los_Angeles
- Organizer: Golden Coast Festival Productions (fictional)
- Vendor: Taco Libre (fictional), booth B-02

## 1. Scope

Vendor sells food and non-alcoholic drinks only. No alcohol.

## 2. Payment collection

2.1 Card payments are collected centrally through the organizer's processor.
2.2 Cash: No cash accepted.

## 3. Organizer fee

3.1 Commission: 15% of eligible festival sales. Eligible sales exclude sales tax and tips and are reduced by refunds.
3.2 Festival minimum: $500.00 for the entire festival, not per night. If the percentage commission for the whole festival is lower, the final settlement adds the difference. Sales tax and tips are never used to pay it.
3.3 Processor fees: Organizer bears card processing fees; they are not deducted from the vendor settlement.

## 4. Settlement

4.1 The organizer issues a statement for each festival business day by 9:00 AM Pacific the following morning and releases any payout (simulated in this demo) from 10:00 AM, subject to controls.
4.2 Earlier nights assess the cumulative percentage less commission already assessed.
4.3 Any amount the vendor still owes after eligible proceeds is recorded as an unpaid balance for collection or waiver. No negative payouts.
